About us

An academy named after an engineer.

Ismail al-Jazari, a twelfth-century engineer, is often called the father of robotics. He built one of the first programmable machines, a musical automaton you reprogrammed by moving pegs on a drum, then wrote the book that let others build it too. That is our whole idea: make something that works, then hand over the drawings.

Why we exist

There is no shortage of AI content. There is a shortage of people who can navigate it.

Awareness is everywhere. Judgement is not.

Most people in Egypt have watched the videos. Free courses are everywhere and the state is running training at national scale. What almost nobody teaches is how to tell a real use from a distraction, which model belongs on which problem, and what to do on Monday morning. That judgement is what an academy should produce.

We organise around the work, not the discipline

Other providers teach data science, or machine learning, or programming. A marketer, a finance lead and an engineer need completely different things from AI, and so does a founder deciding whether to invest in it at all. Our content is built around the work someone actually does.
