About us

An academy named after an Arab engineer.

Ismail al-Jazari was an engineer of the Arab and Islamic golden age, and is often called the father of robotics. In the twelfth century he built some of the first programmable machines, then wrote, in Arabic, the book that let others build them too.

We carry his name because our history is not behind us. The civilisation that gave the world algebra, the algorithm and al-Jazari's machines is now building AI in its own language, and we mean to help it lead rather than follow. That is our whole idea: make something that works, then hand over the drawings.

Why we exist

There is no shortage of AI content. There is a shortage of people who can navigate it.

Awareness is everywhere. Judgement is not.

Most people in Egypt have watched the videos. Free courses are everywhere and the state is running training at national scale. What almost nobody teaches is how to tell a real use from a distraction, which model belongs on which problem, and what to do on Monday morning. That judgement is what an academy should produce.

We organise around the work, not the discipline

Other providers teach data science, or machine learning, or programming. A marketer, a finance lead and an engineer need completely different things from AI, and so does a founder deciding whether to invest in it at all. Our content is built around the work someone actually does.

How our content is built

Function first. Industry always.

Generic courses teach the tools. We teach what a specific role does with them, in the setting that role actually works in.

Built around your function

We do not teach AI in general. We teach AI for marketing, finance, HR, operations and sales: the tasks that fill that role's week, the tools that fit them, and the judgement about what to hand to a model and what to keep. Every track ends with something working that the role will use on Monday.

Set in your industry

Worked examples, data and cases come from your sector: real estate, manufacturing and logistics, outsourcing and customer operations, healthcare, retail. A finance team in a factory closes the month differently from one in a bank, so the examples, the risks and the rules change with it.
